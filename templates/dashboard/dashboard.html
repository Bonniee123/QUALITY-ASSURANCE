{% comment %} No change badge here. This card counts the whole archive, and a lifetime total has no period to be compared against -- the badge was measuring the last 30 days against the 30 before them, while the number beside it measured everything ever uploaded and the line below it measured the last 30 days again. Three time frames, one tile, and two of them invisible. The comparison belongs on "Uploaded This Week", where a period is the point; the 30-day figure stays in the footer, where it is labelled. {% endcomment %}
